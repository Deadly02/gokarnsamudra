Gokarna Travel Guide

This Gokarna travel guide covers beaches, things to do and how to reach. It also covers the best time to visit and a 2-day itinerary. We are a short walk from Gokarna Main Beach.

Last updated 18 November 2026 · By Gokarna Samudra Beach Resort

Beaches in Gokarna

Gokarna has five well-known beaches along a short stretch of coast. Each has a different feel, from busy town sand to quiet coves.

Gokarna Main Beach

Gokarna Main Beach sits right next to the town and Mahabaleshwar Temple. Pilgrims often bathe here before visiting the temple. It is a short walk from our resort. Evenings here are popular with families.

Kudle Beach

Kudle Beach lies south of the town, over a small headland. It has a long curve of sand with cafes and guesthouses. You can reach it by road or on foot from the town. Many travellers stay on for the sunset.

Om Beach

Om Beach is named for its shape, which looks like the Om symbol from above. It is one of the most visited beaches in Gokarna. Boat rides are often offered in season. Swim only where it is marked safe.

Half Moon Beach

Half Moon Beach is a small crescent cove south of Om Beach. You reach it by a trek over rocks or by boat. Facilities are basic, so carry water and snacks.

Paradise Beach

Paradise Beach lies beyond Half Moon Beach. It is rocky and remote, and easiest to reach by boat. A longer trek also leads there. Plan to return well before dark.

Things to Do in Gokarna

Here are simple things to do in Gokarna across two or three days. Mix temple visits, beach walks and slow evenings.

Visit Mahabaleshwar Temple and Koti Tirtha

Start in the old town at Mahabaleshwar Temple. Then walk to Koti Tirtha, the sacred temple tank. Check dress rules and timings before you go. Our page on how to stay near Mahabaleshwar Temple has more tips.

Walk the Gokarna Beach Trek

This coastal trail links Kudle, Om, Half Moon and Paradise beaches. The path climbs over headlands and rocky sections. Start early, wear good shoes and carry water. Many walkers trek one way and return by boat.

Take a Boat Ride

Boats often run between Om, Half Moon and Paradise beaches in season. Agree the fare and return time before you board. Skip boats when the sea is rough.

Explore the Temple Town

Walk Car Street, the main street of old Gokarna. You will find small shops, puja stalls and local snacks. Evenings are lively around the temple.

Day Trips: Mirjan Fort and Yana

Mirjan Fort is a historic laterite fort near Kumta. Yana is known for its tall black limestone rock formations. Both make good half-day or full-day trips by road.

How to Reach Gokarna

Gokarna is on the Karnataka coast in Uttara Kannada district. Most routes combine rail or air with a final road leg.

From Bangalore

Bengaluru to Gokarna is a long overnight journey. Overnight buses run to Gokarna and nearby Kumta. Check trains from Bengaluru to Gokarna Road or Kumta. By car, plan a full day with breaks. Use a maps app for the latest route. See our 2-day plan for Bangalore travellers.

From Goa

From Goa, drive south on NH66 through Karwar and Ankola. Konkan Railway trains link Madgaon with Gokarna Road. Check which trains stop there before you book. Buses also run from Goa towards Karwar and Gokarna.

From Mumbai

From Mumbai, Konkan Railway trains run down the coast towards Gokarna Road, Kumta and Ankola. Check which trains stop at your station. You can also fly to Goa and continue by road or rail.

From Hubli

Hubballi has a domestic airport and a major railway junction. From Hubballi, travel west to the coast by bus, taxi or car.

By Train: Gokarna Road Railway Station

Gokarna Road railway station is on the Konkan Railway. It lies outside the town, so plan a taxi or auto for the last stretch. Kumta and Ankola stations are useful options too.

By Air: Nearest Airport to Gokarna

There is no airport in Gokarna itself. The main options are Goa (Dabolim and Mopa), Hubballi and Mangaluru. Each needs a road or rail transfer to Gokarna.

Best Time to Visit Gokarna

October to March

This is the most popular season. Skies are clearer, the sea is calmer and beach cafes are open. Book early for weekends, Christmas and New Year.

April and May

Days are hot and humid. Plan beach time for early mornings and evenings. A pool helps on the hottest afternoons.

June to September: Gokarna in Monsoon

Heavy rain turns the hills green. The sea is rough and swimming is unsafe. Read our guide to what to expect in the rainy season.

Festivals

Maha Shivaratri at Mahabaleshwar Temple usually falls in February or March. Expect large crowds and book rooms well ahead.

Gokarna 2-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Temple, Town and Main Beach

Arrive in the morning and ask the front desk about an early bag drop. Visit Mahabaleshwar Temple and Koti Tirtha before the midday heat. Check in at 12:00 and cool off in the pool. Walk to Gokarna Main Beach for the evening. End the day with dinner at Ocean Deck Restaurant.

Day 2: Kudle, Om and the Coast

Start early with the trek from Kudle Beach to Om Beach. Return in time to check out by 11:00. Spend the afternoon at Kudle or Om, or take a boat to Half Moon. Head home on an evening bus or train.

Stay Close to the Beach and Temple

A central base saves time in Gokarna. Our resort is a short walk from Gokarna Main Beach. It has a swimming pool, free Wi-Fi, room service and free parking. Ocean Deck Restaurant and Samudra Hall are on site.

Gokarna Travel Questions

Which are the main beaches in Gokarna?

Gokarna Main Beach sits next to the town and temple. Kudle Beach and Om Beach lie to the south. Half Moon Beach and Paradise Beach are further along the coast. You can reach them by trek or by boat. Check sea conditions before you swim.

What is the Gokarna beach trek?

This coastal trail links Kudle, Om, Half Moon and Paradise beaches. The trail follows cliffs and rocky paths along the coast. Start early, carry water and wear good shoes. Avoid the trail in heavy rain. Some travellers walk one way and return by boat.

How do I reach Gokarna from Bangalore?

You can travel by overnight bus, train or car. Check trains to Gokarna Road or Kumta on the Konkan line. Overnight buses run to Gokarna and nearby Kumta. By car, plan a full day's drive with rest breaks. Our 2-day Gokarna itinerary from Bangalore has a simple plan.

Which is the nearest airport to Gokarna?

Goa has two airports, Dabolim and Mopa. Hubballi Airport and Mangaluru International Airport are other options. Each needs a long road transfer to Gokarna. Choose based on flight times and fares from your city. You can also continue by train to Gokarna Road station.

What is the best time to visit Gokarna?

October to March is the most popular season. The weather is drier and the sea is calmer. April and May are hot and humid. The monsoon runs roughly from June to September, with heavy rain. Plan temple visits around festival dates if you want to see them.
