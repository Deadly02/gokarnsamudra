Trip Planning Questions

What is the best time to visit Gokarna?

October to March is the most popular season. The weather is drier and the sea is calmer. April and May are hot and humid. The monsoon runs roughly from June to September, with heavy rain. Plan temple visits around festival dates if you want to see them.

How do I reach Gokarna from Bangalore?

You can travel by overnight bus, train or car. Check trains to Gokarna Road or Kumta on the Konkan line. Overnight buses run to Gokarna and nearby Kumta. By car, plan a full day's drive with rest breaks. Our 2-day Gokarna itinerary from Bangalore has a simple plan.

Is Gokarna good to visit in monsoon?

The monsoon brings green hills, quiet streets and heavy rain. The sea is rough and swimming is unsafe. Many beach shacks close for the season. Temple visits and short town walks are still possible. Pack rain gear and check the forecast before you travel.
