A Gokarna Beach Resort Close to the Sea and the Temple

Gokarna Samudra Beach Resort is on Belehittal Rd, Dandebagh, in Gokarna, Karnataka. Gokarna Main Beach is a short walk away. Mahabaleshwar Temple and Koti Tirtha are close by too. You can mix beach time, temple visits and pool time in one easy stay.

Comparing resorts near Gokarna beach? Location, a pool and on-site food are worth checking. We offer all three, along with free parking and free Wi-Fi. Call or WhatsApp us for directions or help choosing a room.

Where to Stay in Gokarna: Our Location

Wondering where to stay in Gokarna? Staying near the beach and the old town saves travel time. Our resort is a handy hotel near Gokarna Main Beach. Mahabaleshwar Temple is a short walk away. Kudle Beach and Om Beach lie further south, by road or trek.

Questions About Staying With Us

Is there a resort in Gokarna with a swimming pool close to Main Beach?

Yes. Gokarna Samudra Beach Resort has an on-site swimming pool. The resort is a short walk from Gokarna Main Beach. Pool Front Cottages have a private sit-out overlooking the pool. You can swim at the resort and walk to the beach on the same day.

Can I stay here to visit Mahabaleshwar Temple?

Yes. The resort is a short walk from Mahabaleshwar Temple. It suits pilgrims and families who want to stay close to the temple. You can rest by the pool after darshan. Jain food is available on request at Ocean Deck Restaurant. Book early for festival dates such as Shivaratri.

Is Gokarna Samudra a family resort with a restaurant?

Yes. Deluxe Rooms are spacious, with a balcony and scenic views, and suit families. Ocean Deck Restaurant is on site. It serves Indian, South Indian, Chinese, Continental and Pan-Asian food. The resort also has a swimming pool, free Wi-Fi, room service and free parking.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is at 12:00 noon. Check-out is at 11:00 am. Pets are not allowed at the resort. For special timing requests, call or WhatsApp +91 77608 50555 before you arrive. The team will tell you what is possible. Free parking is available on site.

How do I book direct with the resort?

Use the Book Now button on any page. It opens our official booking engine to check rates and availability. You can also call or WhatsApp +91 77608 50555. Or email reservations@gokarnasamudra.com with your dates and number of guests. Booking direct lets you speak to the resort team about your needs.
