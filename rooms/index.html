Questions About Our Rooms

Which room types are available?

We offer three room types. Standard Rooms are cozy, with modern amenities, for solo travellers and couples. Deluxe Rooms have premium furniture, a balcony and scenic views, for families. Pool Front Cottages have a private sit-out overlooking the pool. Compare them on our rooms page.

Is Gokarna Samudra a budget resort in Gokarna?

Standard Rooms are cozy and have modern amenities. Prices can change with season and dates. Compare all room types on the booking page. You will see the exact rate before you confirm. Booking direct also lets you ask the team about rates.

Which room suits families?

The Deluxe Room suits families. It is spacious, with premium furniture, a balcony and scenic views. Families can also use the pool and Ocean Deck Restaurant on site. Free parking helps if you are driving with children and luggage. Jain food is available on request.

Are pets allowed at the resort?

No. Pets are not allowed at Gokarna Samudra Beach Resort. This applies to all room types, including Pool Front Cottages. Please plan pet care before you travel. If you have an assistance animal, contact the team before booking. Call +91 77608 50555 or email reservations@gokarnasamudra.com.
